# Reference catalog: the editing patterns behind VidRush-style documentaries

Read off five finished VidRush videos (20 to 25 minutes each, 3.5 to 3.8 s
average shot, 300 to 425 cuts) by sampling frames every 4 s and 2-fps bursts
around every graphic. This records the *patterns* (what, when, how long, how
it enters, holds and leaves, what sound goes with it) so ThumbGenius can build
its own components with the same editing grammar. No assets are copied.

## The grammar in one paragraph

Footage carries about 70% of the runtime. Roughly every 20 to 40 seconds the
edit leaves the footage for 3 to 8 seconds of a *treatment*: a label riding
on the footage (most common), a text card, a map, a data graphic, a photo
treatment or a document card, then returns. Treatments are chosen by what the
line says, not on a timer: a number gets a number; a place gets a map or a
location card; a date gets a date card; a person gets a person card; a quote
gets a quote card; a chapter change gets a chapter title with a ghost word
behind it. Transitions are almost always hard cuts; a light leak, a split
wipe or a glitch appears once every few minutes, at a section change.
Sounds are sparse: one every two to three minutes, tied to a graphic
(typewriter for typed text, a soft impact for a date or chapter, a whoosh for
a map or a drawn line, a pop for a timeline node), 20 to 35% volume.

## Patterns

Duration is the on-screen hold; "enter" is what moves in the first 0.3 to 1 s.

### Text on footage

| Pattern | Used when | Duration | Enter | Hold | Exit | Type | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Label pill ("930 · FEET", "AGE 1") | a measurement, an age, a count spoken | 3 to 4 s | pill scales in 0.3 s, text types 0.5 s | still | cut | typewriter caps | none |
| Dual label boxes ("SPONGE: ABSORBS" / "PAVEMENT: SHEDS") | two things contrasted in one line | 4 to 5 s | first box, dotted connector draws 0.6 s, second box | still | cut | typewriter caps in dark boxes | none |
| Red-bar label ("Boston, July 27, 2004") | a place and date said in passing | 3 s | bar drops, text types | still | cut | typewriter | none |
| Highlight sentence | the thesis of a section, spoken slowly | 4 to 6 s | words appear left to right, key words get a red or yellow highlight box 0.2 s after | still | cut | italic script inside the highlight | none |
| Key-phrase kinetic ("THE WORLD WANTED / **OBAMA**", "Then fame came / **back**") | an emotional line with one strong word | 5 to 8 s | phrase by phrase every 1 to 1.5 s, the strong word bolder and larger | still | cut | bold condensed caps + italic sans | none |
| Typed title ("Administrative Exclusion") | a term of art or a verdict | 3 to 4 s | types in 1 s with cursor | still | cut | large typewriter | typewriter 25% |
| Ghost chapter ("Eight Children, Four Separate Worlds") | a section starts | 4 to 5 s | title fades up 0.4 s, giant translucent word behind it slides slowly | slow drift | cut | bold sans or serif; ghost word 4x size at 8% opacity | soft impact 30% |
| Warning / status caps ("SCIOTO FLOOD WARN", "BANKFULL / IN CHANNEL") | an alert level or state is named | 3 s | stencil types or bar slides | still | cut | distressed stencil or caps in a bar | none |

### Numbers and data

| Pattern | Used when | Duration | Enter | Hold | Exit | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Big number ("$2,500,000", "137 Thousand") | the line's whole point is one figure | 3 to 4 s | types in or counts up 1 s | still | cut | soft impact 30% |
| Percent gauge (vertical thermometer + "40%") | a share or risk percentage | 5 to 6 s | card fades from footage 0.5 s, bar fills and counter counts 1.5 s | still, source line below | cut | none |
| Bar chart (2 to 4 horizontal or vertical bars) | two or more values compared | 6 to 8 s | hard cut to black, title types, axes fade, bars grow staggered 1.5 s | still | cut | none |
| Ruler gauge (river stage with thresholds) | a level against thresholds | 5 s | ruler draws, markers slide to their values | still | cut | none |
| Pictogram counter (rows of triangles filling, some turning red) | "N of M" or a count of people, homes, warnings | 6 s | pictograms appear in rows 2 s, the counted share recolours row by row | still | cut | none |
| Timeline (nodes on a line, labels above and below) | a sequence of dated events | 6 to 8 s | line and nodes draw left to right 3 s, labels type at each node | still | cut | pop 35% per node |
| Numbered steps ("1 Arrived With Little / 2 Degree in 3 Years") | a process or a chain of decisions | 6 s | dots and a curve connect, each label types | still | cut | pop |
| Icon row (3 circular badges + pill labels) | three categories or severities | 5 s | badges scale in one by one 0.5 s apart | still | cut | pop |

### Maps

| Pattern | Used when | Duration | Enter | Hold | Exit | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arc route on a dark world map | travel, shipping, migration between two named places | 5 to 8 s | cut to map, arc draws 3 to 4 s while the camera pans slowly; label pills at each end; optionally a photo thumbnail rides the arc | camera keeps drifting | cut | whoosh 35% |
| Location zoom (world or region to a pin) | a place is introduced | 4 to 5 s | zoom from region to town 2 s, pulse ring at the pin, rotated label settles beside it | slow push | cut | map ping |
| Region highlight (state or county fill) | a state, county or country is the subject | 4 s | fill sweeps in 0.6 s, label pill and an icon (wave, flame, warning) pop | still | cut | none |
| Multi-city vector map (dark grey states, red roads, city labels, radar or threat blobs) | several places in one system | 5 to 6 s | labels appear in reading order 0.3 s apart, blobs pulse | slow pan | cut | none |
| River or road polyline draw | a river, aqueduct or route is followed | 4 to 5 s | light-blue or red line draws along the path 3 s, pill label at the end | still | cut | whoosh |
| Location card (satellite inset + typed label + pictogram counter) | a place and date open a story | 6 to 8 s | satellite fills frame with the label, then shrinks to an inset while the counter fills | still | cut | none |
| Split map / panel (map left, checklist or photo right) | a place and its facts together | 5 s | map cuts in, panel slides from the right, lines reveal one by one | still | cut | none |

### Photos, documents and people

| Pattern | Used when | Duration | Enter | Hold | Exit | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Framed photo (film frame, polaroid, torn paper) with typed caption | one archival photograph | 4 s | photo scales in 0.4 s, light leak sweeps once, caption types | slow push | cut | none |
| Photo pair / before-after (arrow between) | two photos compared | 5 to 6 s | first photo, red arrow draws, second slides in after 2 s | still | cut | none |
| Photo stack | many photos from one era | 6 to 8 s | white-bordered photos drop on the pile every 1 to 1.5 s at slight rotations | still | cut | paper 35% |
| Person card (cutout on a coloured disc, name types, role in small caps) | a person is introduced | 4 to 5 s | cutout slides in 0.5 s, name types 1 s, role fades | still | cut or dissolve to footage | typewriter 25% |
| Article / archival record card (paper, masthead, red highlights) | a document or newspaper is cited | 6 to 8 s | card fades in, headline types, body types with highlights | slow push | cut | paper or typewriter |
| Document mock (form title, seal, ruled lines, yellow highlighter strip) | a legal or official document | 4 to 5 s | card fades, highlighter sweeps 0.8 s | still | cut | none |
| Annotation on footage (cream or dark box + pointer line to the subject) | a thing on screen is named | 3 to 4 s | box scales in, line draws to the point 0.4 s | still | cut | none |
| Red rectangle highlight on footage | "this" building, lot or area | 3 s | rectangle draws 0.5 s, caption types | still | cut | none |
| Green or blurred matte behind 4:3 material | any non-16:9 photo or clip | whole shot | none | subtle drift | cut | none |

### Transitions (rare, at section changes)

| Pattern | Frequency | Duration | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hard cut | almost every cut | 0 | the default |
| Orange or warm light leak at the edge | every 3 to 5 minutes | 0.6 to 1 s | over the outgoing shot's last frames |
| Split wipe (right half slides in) | every 5 to 8 minutes | 0.5 s | often with a red tag on the incoming half |
| Glitch or VHS tear | once or twice a video | 0.3 s | between eras |
| Orange or blue vertical bar wipe | once a video | 0.5 s | into a chapter title |
| Pixel mosaic | once a video | 0.5 s | into archival material |
| Colour wash (blue) | once a video | 0.7 s | into a quote |

### Rhythm rules observed

- A treatment every 20 to 40 s of runtime; never two graphics back to back
  unless they are one sequence (photo pair, numbered steps).
- Labels ride on footage and do not interrupt it; cards replace footage and
  are held 4 to 8 s, then a hard cut back to footage.
- The same treatment family is not repeated within about 60 s; the same
  *look* (colour, typeface) is kept for the whole video.
- Captions are phrase-length, centred at the bottom, white sans, no
  word-by-word karaoke; they are absent during cards that carry their own
  text.
- Music sits far under the narration and changes only at section starts.
