went through 3 of its 13 proxies. With ~11 % a try and ~3.7 tries, a line finds a clip about 1 time in 3 - and
that is what happened (51 of 195 lines in pass 1). The proxies did **not** lose clips (98 % of downloads worked);
they and the CPU made each try slow. The empty lines were then filled with spare *pictures* before spare clips
were exhausted, and nothing stopped stills from following stills.
