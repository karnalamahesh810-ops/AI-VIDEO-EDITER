## 1. Why the video had so few clips (the funnel)

**Measured on the finished timeline:** clips **42.3 %** of the screen time (85 scenes, 451 s), stills **57.3 %**
(118 scenes, 611 s), one animation. 96 of the 175 lines the planner asked footage for ended as a still.
Stills are chained: 32 runs of two or more stills in a row; the longest is **8 stills, 38.8 s** (16:01-16:40),
then 6 stills / 31.7 s (5:20) and three runs of 5. A still is on screen 5.2 s on average; 115 of 118 stay over 3 s.

**The clip funnel of the footage search (195 lines):**

| stage | in | out | lost | what was lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| search + metadata | ~8 scouted candidates a line (1,570 storyboard picks) | 744 download tries | ~53 % of scouted candidates | storyboard pick "no tile fits" / low score, vertical / old / AI-story uploads, moments already shown in earlier videos; 53 more turned down as games, music videos, dramas, kids' cartoons |
| download (YouTube sections) | 744 | 730 ok | **1.9 %** | bot check 9 (1.2 %), googlevideo 403 STREAM_REFUSED 5 (0.7 %); ERANGE 0, timeouts 0, paid 0, empty files 0 |
| cut / local filters | 600 (pass 1) | 540 gated | ~10 % | no clean stretch long enough, burned-in text, upscaled (soft) upload |
| AI-slop gate | 540 | ~217 | **57 %** | AI-painted 119 (22 %), slideshow of stills 82 (15 %), still with a slow pan 56 (10 %), other creators' captions 21, TV map / live stream 14, frozen 11, studio 6, game 1; soft 14 |
| vision judge | 324 judged | ~85 placed | ~74 % | text or watermark 145 (45 % of judged), studio / talking head 60 (19 %), AI 5, too poor 5; the rest off this line (kept in the spare bank: 180 clips banked) |
| time box | 300 s a line, clip stages get 65 % | ~3.7 downloads a line | - | each candidate cost ~32 s (26 s download + 6 s gate) on a CPU-saturated pod; pass 1 ended on its own at 1,495 s of a 2,340 s box |
| fill | 49 lines still empty | 44 filled | - | spare clips 12, **spare pictures 25**, other moments 6, a picture 1, left 5; the 14-photo cap held 65 photos, 64 came back |

Proxies (per proxy, events): proxy_08 = Decodo 10015: 240 sections ok, 4 bot checks, 3 x 403;
proxy_11 = 10020: 270 ok, 1 bot check; proxy_13 = Webshare 198.145.103.89: 220 ok, 4 bot checks, 2 x 403.
**The other 10 never carried a section.** All 10 took a bot check on a metadata read between 08:20:11 and 08:20:20,
the job's first 9 seconds (18 of the job's 31 metadata failures). Five (proxy_01, 02, 05, 06, 12) were left
"degraded" by one refusal - the rule live worker 8b570fd fixed. Five (proxy_03, 04, 07, 09, 10) took a second
refusal, were quarantined for 5 minutes and then sat **"recovering" for the next 40 minutes without a single trial
request** - a recovering route ranked behind every healthy route and so never got its trial (fixed here).

**Main cause, in plain words.** For this story YouTube is mostly junk: only about **1 in 9 downloaded clips was
usable** (more than half were AI-made pictures, slideshows of stills or still photos with a slow zoom; most of the
rest carried another channel's text or watermark or showed a studio). Each line had time for only about
**3-4 tries**, because every try took ~32 s on a pod whose CPU was full of x264 encodes and whose YouTube traffic
went through 3 of its 13 proxies. With ~11 % a try and ~3.7 tries, a line finds a clip about 1 time in 3 - and
that is what happened (51 of 195 lines in pass 1). The proxies did **not** lose clips (98 % of downloads worked);
they and the CPU made each try slow. The empty lines were then filled with spare *pictures* before spare clips
were exhausted, and nothing stopped stills from following stills.
