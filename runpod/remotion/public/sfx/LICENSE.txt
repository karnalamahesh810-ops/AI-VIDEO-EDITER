Sound effects from Mixkit (https://mixkit.co), used under the Mixkit License
(free for commercial and personal use in video projects). IDs: typewriter 1372,
whoosh 1489, paper 1530, impact 1143, pop 2358, glitch 2595, bell 933, page 1101.
Trimmed to 2.5 s and loudness-normalised (-16 LUFS).

Added 2026-09-27 by the project owner (their own licensed files): "whoosh-cinematic-for-maps.mp3",
"typing text.mp3", "glitch fx transistion.mp3". The renderer plays the loudness-matched copies
map-whoosh.mp3, typewriter.mp3 (first 3.5 s of the typing file) and glitch-transition.mp3.
bell.mp3 (Mixkit 933) was removed; map pings now use map-whoosh.mp3.

Added 2026-09-29 (transitions + sound pass): keys, whoosh-soft, swipe, boom-soft, glitch-short,
riser-short and paper-slide are edits of the files above (typing text, whoosh, impact,
glitch fx transistion, riser, page: trimmed, filtered, re-timed). click, tick, count-tick, ding,
shutter, flash-hit and marker are synthesised from scratch (numpy sine/noise), no third-party audio.
Every mp3 here was then loudness-matched to about -21 LUFS max momentary (all within ~3 dB),
true peak <= -3 dBFS. sfx_meta.json lists each file's duration and the time of its loudest point
("peak", seconds from the start) so the planner can land that point on the visual hit.

Added 2026-09-30 (premium sound pass): 42 sounds generated with ElevenLabs sound effects
(eleven_text_to_sound v2, the owner's pay-as-you-go account, which allows commercial use), then
trimmed (onset within a few ms), faded, DC/subsonic-filtered, narrowed where L/R cancelled in mono,
loudness-matched to about -20 LUFS max momentary (short clicks sit lower: the true-peak cap of
-3.5 dBFS binds first; "lufs" in sfx_meta.json) and encoded MP3 192 kbps 44.1 kHz stereo.
Sound-designed from those takes: riser-short-v2 (rising sweep into the impact-punch hit),
zoom-in-whoosh (reversed whoosh into a soft thump), count-final (lock click with a low body),
ui-tick and letter-tick (single ticks cut from tick takes), paper-tear (a take of tape-rip).
New names never overwrite an older file; "-v2" marks the better take of an existing name.

2026-10-01 (sounds never over the narration): every file's "lufs" in sfx_meta.json is now measured the same
way - its loudest 400 ms (BS.1770 momentary loudness: K-weighted at 48 kHz, a 10 ms hop, the file padded
with silence) - and "peakDb" is its sample peak (dBFS) at its own rate. src/sfxplan.py levels each sound from
it: a file hotter than the -20 LUFS reference is turned down by the difference, a quieter one is never raised.
